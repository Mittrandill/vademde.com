Vademde 1.0.5

• Credit card statements are now recorded transaction by transaction: when you scan a statement, each purchase is added to the card and to Transactions with its own date, while the total balance is still tracked separately.
• Automatic categories: each purchase on the statement is categorized based on the merchant name (e.g. café, groceries, fuel, subscriptions). You see the suggestions before saving and can change any of them.
• A simpler confirmation screen: when purchases are categorized individually, you're no longer asked for a separate general category for the statement.
• Minor improvements and bug fixes.
