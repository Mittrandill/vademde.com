Vademde 1.0.4

• Refreshed welcome: a new animated onboarding flow that shows you how to scan a document and what you can do next.
• Payment receipts (Plus): attach a receipt, photo, or PDF to any payment; open it in one tap from the payment row's paperclip icon. When you scan a bank transfer receipt, Vademde reads the sender, recipient, IBAN, and amount, and suggests the matching debt — the payment is only recorded once you confirm.
• Document Archive (Plus): all your receipts in one place. Search, browse by month, or reach them straight from a contact's page.
• New paywall: compare the Plus and Business plans. Eligible accounts get a 7-day free trial.
• New version notice: the app now tells you when a newer version is available.
• New Make Payment and Receive Payment screens: from a contact's page or the + menu in Transactions, choose which invoices/debts to settle and pay by cash, bank transfer, credit card, cheque, or promissory note. Partial payments are supported; the rest of the invoice stays open.
• Paying by cheque or promissory note now settles the invoice: give a 20,000 TL cheque against a 30,000 TL invoice and the invoice drops to 10,000 TL while the cheque is tracked with its own due date — the debt is no longer counted twice. When the cheque is paid on its due date, the money leaves the account you choose. Scanned cheques/notes also ask which invoice they cover.
• Payments on installment records now go to the installments: payments on notes, loans, salaries, and rent are applied starting from the oldest open installment, so the calendar and reminders stay accurate.
• "Pay" in the calendar now asks which account you paid from; credit card debts are paid from the card page.
• Transactions linked to a payment can no longer be edited or deleted by mistake; changes are made from the record's payment history.
• Account selection is now required for payments: every payment is now recorded as a real account transaction, so your account balance and Transactions list show it correctly.
• Redesigned receipt confirmation screen: brought in line with the other document confirmation screens; if the person on the receipt isn't a saved contact, you're offered to create one, and if the bank account isn't registered, you're offered to add it.
• Upload a document instead of filling out the form: a shortcut pointing you to the camera scanner was added to the New Transaction and New Debt/Receivable screens.
• Gold records fixed: quarter, half, and full gold units now display and save with the correct amounts; a raw internal code (e.g. ceyrek_altin) that sometimes appeared in the account detail's Currency field has also been fixed.
• The balance card on the home screen now displays correctly at larger text sizes.
• Contact and account balances now update instantly after a transaction is recorded, without needing to leave and reopen the page.
• Minor improvements and bug fixes.
