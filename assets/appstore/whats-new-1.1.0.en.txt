Vademde 1.1.0

• Voice entry: Describe it and the assistant recognizes cheques, invoices, loans, expenses, collections or transfers between accounts, then fills in the form for you to approve.
• The assistant asks for what is missing, such as the amount, due date, account or related vehicle.
• New + button: Scan a document or record by voice.
• Cheque and promissory note serial numbers now have their own field.
• Faster document scanning, with more accurate drawer, payee, buyer and seller detection.

Voice entry is available on Plus and Business plans, and each voice entry uses 1 of your monthly scans. A record is created only when you approve it, and Vademde does not store voice recordings.
