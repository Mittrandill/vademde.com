Vademde 1.0.6

• All-new design: Every screen has been redrawn with a cleaner, iOS-native look. What you owe is purple, what you're owed is green; yellow is only for actions. The welcome screens are new too.
• Card statements, line by line: Scan a statement and every purchase is added to the card and to Transactions with its own date, automatically categorized by merchant. Review the suggestions and change anything before saving.
• Redesigned credit card page: Current balance, limit, due date, statements and installments on one screen; pay your card directly from here.
• Card installments: Track how installment purchases weigh on your upcoming statements.
• Cheque and note portfolio: Keep received cheques in your portfolio, endorsed or deposited for collection; mark bounced ones. Each cheque's journey is shown on its detail page.
• Due timeline: Payments and collections for the next 7, 30 and 90 days on the home screen.
• Quick add: Long-press the Scan button to add an expense, income, transfer or scheduled record.
• e-Invoice QR code: Scan the QR code on an invoice and the details fill in automatically.
• No document lost when your quota is full: Scanned documents are saved as drafts so you can process them later from the home screen.
• Redesigned reports: Preset periods and custom date ranges, change versus the previous period, and section selection for PDF export.
• Transaction filters: Filter by date, type, account, category, contact and amount range.
• Subscriptions and Currency & gold: See your monthly subscription total, upcoming renewals, the TRY value of your holdings and live rates.
• Cash alert: Get notified in advance when an account may go negative in the coming days.
• Payment reminders: Send a reminder via WhatsApp, SMS or email from a contact's page.
• Contact statements now include foreign-currency records.
• Unsaved-changes warning on forms; an info banner when you're offline.
• If the app lock can't be opened, you can sign in again with your email and password.
• Smart insights and Ask (Plus): Saving suggestions based on your records and answers to your questions.
• More accurate numbers: Fixes for overpayments, overdue days and exchange rates on foreign-currency transactions; payment records are more reliable.
• Minor improvements and bug fixes.
