Vademde 1.0.6

• All-new design: new colors, new typefaces and redrawn screens. What you owe is purple, what you're owed is green; yellow is only for actions.
• Due-date timeline: see payments and collections for the next 7, 30 and 90 days on a single line.
• Quick add: long-press the Scan button to add an expense, income, transfer or dated record.
• Cheque and promissory note portfolio: track received cheques in your portfolio, endorsed or sent for collection, and flag the ones that bounce.
• My subscriptions and Currency & gold: see your monthly subscription total, upcoming renewals, the value of your foreign-currency and gold holdings and live rates.
• Cash alerts: get warned in advance when an account may go negative.
• Card installments: follow how installment purchases weigh on upcoming statements.
• e-Invoice QR code: scan the QR code on an invoice and the form fills itself in.
• Receivable reminders: send a reminder via WhatsApp, SMS or email.
• Smart suggestions and Ask (Plus): savings suggestions and answers to your questions, based on your own records.
• Minor improvements and bug fixes.
